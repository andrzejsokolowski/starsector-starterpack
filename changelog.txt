Version 1.6.0 (2026-11-20)
- New switch next to SAVE A SNAPSHOT: "Save known blueprints and hull mods". On by default. Turn it off and the snapshot leaves your known ship, fighter and weapon blueprints and learned hull mods behind, so a new game started from it has to find them again. The console has "starterpack snapshot blueprints on" and "off".

Version 1.5.1 (2026-11-20)
- Snapshots moved from their own tab on the Manage (Y) screen to its shared Others tab, under StarterPack. Needs OddiszCommons 1.1.0 or newer; with an older one, use "starterpack snapshot" in the console.

Version 1.5.0 (2026-11-20)
- Snapshots, a new game plus: save a running campaign and start new games from it. A snapshot keeps your fleet with its loadouts, officers and AI core captains, mothballed ships, cargo, credits, level, skills, unspent and story points, faction standings, hotbar, and the blueprints and hull mods you know. Ships start repaired.
- Save one on the StarterPack tab of the Manage (Y) screen when OddiszCommons is installed (optional), or with "starterpack snapshot" in the console.
- Snapshots are kept apart from templates, on a new SNAPSHOTS tab in the main menu window: see what each holds, rename, delete, or start every new game from one. That snapshot applies instead of the active template.
- Fixed ships with D-mods not coming back from the refit bench.

Version 1.3.0 (2026-10-31)
- Templates can set your starting standing with factions, on the CHARACTER tab: one value for every faction the intel screen lists, and single factions on top of it (for example everyone at 0 but the pirates at -50). Hidden factions such as the Remnant can be added one by one.

Version 1.2.0 (2026-10-22)
- Modules of multi-module ships can be refitted. Open a module in the editor and fit it like a ship, or fit it on its tab in the refit bench, and the pack starts the ship with that loadout.
- Fixed modules getting swapped between multi-module ships, such as an Anthracite getting a Neoyselif armour plate for an arm, or two left arms. Templates that already picked up a wrong module go back to the right one.
- Fixed the refit bench reloading its last saved loadouts over the active template every time the game started.
