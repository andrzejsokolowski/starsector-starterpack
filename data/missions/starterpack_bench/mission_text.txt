This is not a battle. It is your StarterPack template, standing in a dry dock that happens to be shaped like a mission.

Press REFIT and fit your ships using the game's own refit screen -- real ordnance points, real hullmod rules, real weapon groups. When you are done, leave the mission. StarterPack reads what you built straight back into the template.

Ships are added and removed in the STARTER PACK editor on the main menu. The bench only fits the ones already there.

The enemy is a formality. You are not expected to fight it, and nothing here is scored.
